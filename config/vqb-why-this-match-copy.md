## Purpose

The "Why This Match?" section appears below the primary CTA on the product result card.
It expands on demand (tap or click) to explain why the quiz recommended this product
based on the user's answers.

Its job is to:
- Build trust and credibility.
- Reinforce the personalized nature of the recommendation.
- Use compliant language to explain the product's function without making disease claims.
- Encourage the user to feel confident in the recommendation.

---

## Placement & Behavior

- Triggered by tapping "Why This Match?" secondary CTA.
- Expands inline below the CTA (accordion, no modal).
- Collapsed by default on mobile — keeps the card clean.
- On desktop, can be expanded by default if space allows.

## Prohibited Language — Do Not Use

- stop hair loss / reverse hair loss
- cure anxiety / fix stress
- treat IBS / gut disease
- fix insomnia / cure sleep problems
- guaranteed results
- clinically proven (unless citing a specific clinical study)
- FDA approved
- this will / this does (replace with "designed to" / "formulated to")
