## Purpose

These rules define the visual style, layout, typography, colour, and compliance standards for all Vital Vision Shop image cards generated by Node/sharp or built in Canva templates.

Every card must feel premium, warm, educational, and brand-aligned. Not salesy. Not clinical. Not stock-photo generic.

---

## Card Types

| Type | Format | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q&A Story card | Portrait | 1080 x 1920 px (9:16) | Instagram Stories Q&A |
| Carousel card | Square | 1080 x 1080 px (1:1) | Instagram carousel posts |
| Feed graphic | Square or portrait | 1080 x 1080 or 1080 x 1350 px | Instagram feed posts |
| Reel cover card | Portrait | 1080 x 1920 px (9:16) | Instagram Reel cover |

## Colour Palette

### Primary

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream white | #FAF8F5 | Card backgrounds, primary surface |
| Warm ivory | #F2EDE6 | Secondary background, alternating slides |
| Soft sage | #B8C4B1 | Accent, dividers, subtle highlights |
| Deep forest | #3B4A3F | Primary text, headings |
| Warm sand | #D9C9B0 | Borders, subtle accents |

### Secondary (use sparingly)

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blush rose | #E8C4BB | CTA backgrounds, highlight blocks |
| Warm terracotta | #C4886A | Accent headlines, product-specific callouts |
| Soft gold | #C9A96E | Premium detail, logo accent |

### Never use

- Pure white (#FFFFFF) — too harsh, not on-brand
- Black (#000000) — too stark
- Neon or saturated colours
- Cold grey tones
- High-contrast colour combinations

## Typography

### Font Stack (Canva templates)

| Role | Font | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Cormorant Garamond | Regular or Light | Main Q&A question, card title |
| Subheadline | Jost | Medium | Product name, section label |
| Body | Jost | Regular | Answer text, educational copy |
| Disclaimer | Jost | Light | Small disclaimer line at bottom |
| CTA | Jost | SemiBold | Call to action button or line |

Fallback fonts for Node/sharp generation (system or bundled):
- Headline: any serif — Georgia, Times New Roman, or bundled custom TTF
- Body and CTA: any clean sans-serif — Arial, Helvetica, or bundled custom TTF

### Font Sizes (1080px canvas)

| Role | Size (px) |
|---|---|
| Headline | 64–80px |
| Subheadline | 32–40px |
| Body copy | 28–34px |
| Disclaimer | 18–22px |
| CTA text | 30–36px |

### Typography Rules

- Headline is always the largest element on the card.
- Never use all-caps for body copy — only for short labels or category tags.
- Line height: 1.4–1.6x for body copy, 1.2x for headlines.
- Text must have sufficient contrast against the background (minimum 4.5:1 ratio).
- Left-aligned or centred text — no right-aligned body copy.
- Maximum 60 characters per line for body copy.
- Maximum 45 characters per line for headlines.

## Layout Rules

### Q&A Story Card (1080 x 1920)

```
[ TOP ZONE — 15% of height ]
  Brand logo or product tag (small, top centre or top left)

[ QUESTION ZONE — 35% of height ]
  Question text — large, warm, centred
  Optional: subtle Q&A label above ("You asked:" or "Common question:")

[ DIVIDER — thin warm sand line or sage accent ]

[ ANSWER ZONE — 35% of height ]
  Answer text — body size, left-aligned or centred
  Optional: product soft mention at bottom of answer zone

[ BOTTOM ZONE — 15% of height ]
  Disclaimer (if supplement benefit mentioned)
  CTA line ("Comment BLOOM" or "Link in bio")
  Brand signature: "Self-care starts here."
```

### Carousel Card (1080 x 1080)

```
[ TOP — 12% ]
  Slide number (e.g. 01 / 05) — small, right or left aligned
  Optional: product tag

[ CONTENT ZONE — 76% ]
  Headline or question — top of content zone
  Body copy — below headline
  Optional: icon, simple illustration, or product image (right side)

[ BOTTOM — 12% ]
  Disclaimer if needed
  Brand signature or CTA
```

### Feed Graphic (1080 x 1080 or 1080 x 1350)

```
[ TOP — 10% ]
  Optional: category label or product name

[ CONTENT ZONE — 80% ]
  Large headline — centred or left-aligned
  Subheadline or short body copy below
  Optional: product image or lifestyle image (bottom half)

[ BOTTOM — 10% ]
  Disclaimer if needed
  Brand signature
```

## Spacing and Margin Rules

- Minimum margin from card edge: 80px on all sides (1080px canvas)
- Minimum margin from card edge: 120px on all sides (1920px canvas)
- Internal spacing between text blocks: 24–40px
- Do not let text touch the card edge or bleed off the safe zone.
- Leave bottom 120px clear for Instagram Stories UI overlays.
- Leave top 80px clear for Stories UI elements.

---

## Brand Element Rules

### Logo
- Logo appears small — top centre or top left.
- Never larger than 10% of card width.
- Always on a clean background — no overlap with busy imagery.
- Logo file: [to be added to assets/templates/qa-cards/ by Lucy]

### Product Name Tags
- Product name appears as a small label — e.g. "Inner Bloom" in Jost Medium, sage or forest colour.
- Never the largest element on the card.
- Not required on every card — use when content is product-specific.

### Brand Signature
- "Self-care starts here." appears at the bottom of cards as a small, elegant line.
- Font: Cormorant Garamond, Light or Italic, small size (18–22px).
- Always present on Story cards and Reel covers.
- Optional on carousel slides (required on first and last slide).

## Compliance Rules

Apply config/compliance-rules.md to all card copy.

### Text on cards must never include

- Cure, treat, heal, fix, prevent disease, diagnose
- No more bloating, stop bloating, fix your gut
- Guaranteed results, clinically proven (unless verified)
- Before-and-after language
- Hard outcome promises

### Disclaimer rule

If a card mentions a supplement benefit, include the short disclaimer:

```
Wellness support only. Not medical advice. Results may vary.
```

Disclaimer text: Jost Light, 18–20px, warm sand or sage colour, bottom of card.

### Visual compliance

- No before-and-after imagery on cards
- No clinical or medical imagery (no pills, scales, medical charts)
- No body-shaming visual language
- No exaggerated transformation graphics

## Card Content Rules

### Q&A cards

- Question must reflect real customer language (see config/product-library.md audience language)
- Answer must use safe wellness language (see config/compliance-rules.md)
- Answer is educational, warm, and softly informative — not a sales pitch
- One question and answer per card — do not crowd
- Maximum 3 lines for the question, maximum 5 lines for the answer

### Carousel cards

- Maximum 6–8 slides per carousel
- First slide: hook or question (most important — drives saves and swipes)
- Middle slides: educational points, one idea per slide
- Last slide: soft CTA + disclaimer + brand signature
- Consistent visual style across all slides in the set

### Feed graphics

- Single clear message per card
- Headline does the heavy lifting — body copy is optional and short
- Strong visual hierarchy — headline → subheadline → CTA

## Approved Canva Template Approach

Until Node/sharp automation is ready, Canva templates are the primary production method.

Canva template naming convention:
```
vv-[card-type]-template-v[number].canva
```

Examples:
```
vv-qa-story-template-v1.canva
vv-carousel-template-v1.canva
vv-feed-graphic-template-v1.canva
```

Templates are stored in: assets/templates/qa-cards/

Each template must be built once in Canva following these style rules, then duplicated and text-swapped for each new card set.
