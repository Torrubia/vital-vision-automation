# Vital Vision Shop — Project Context

## Business
Vital Vision Shop is a wellness-focused Shopify store selling natural wellness products and supplements.

## Current Core Products
1. Inner Bloom — Advanced Probiotic Formula
2. Inner Calm — Magnesium Glycinate
3. Inner Grow — Hair, Skin & Nails Support
4. Inner Balance — Daily / Complete Multivitamin

## Main Funnel
Instagram / Facebook / Reels
→ 1-question wellness quiz
→ Personalized product recommendation
→ Product page
→ Checkout
→ Email follow-up

## Brand Voice
Premium, calm, modern, intelligent, feminine, trustworthy, wellness-focused.

## Compliance Rules
Do not make disease claims.
Do not claim to cure, treat, prevent, or diagnose any condition.
Use soft wellness language:
- may support
- helps maintain
- supports overall wellness
- designed to support
- wellness routine
- daily support

Avoid:
- cure
- heal
- guaranteed results
- treats anxiety
- fixes gut disease
- reverses hair loss
- medical promises
- FDA-approved claims unless verified

## Publishing Rules
AUTO_PUBLISH=false
REQUIRE_HUMAN_APPROVAL=true

No content can be published automatically.
All social posts, ad copy, email copy, and website copy must be saved as drafts first.

## Main Goal
Build a safe organic content system first, then validate engagement, then scale with automation.
