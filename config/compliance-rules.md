# Vital Vision Shop Compliance Rules

## Purpose

These rules protect Vital Vision Shop from unsafe supplement claims, medical claims, misleading promises, platform blocks, and compliance issues.

Use these rules for:
- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- Pinterest
- YouTube Shorts
- product pages
- email
- captions
- Stories
- Reels
- DM automation
- organic content drafts

---

## Core Rule

A disclaimer does not make an unsafe claim safe.

The claim must be compliant before the disclaimer is added.

Vital Vision Shop products must be positioned as wellness support, not as cures, treatments, preventions, diagnoses, or guaranteed transformations.

---

## Forbidden Claims

Never say or imply that a product can:
- cure
- treat
- heal
- fix
- prevent disease
- diagnose
- reverse
- eliminate
- guarantee results
- replace medical care
- replace a balanced diet
- work for everyone

---

## Forbidden Phrases

Avoid:
- no more bloating
- stop bloating
- fix your gut
- heal your stomach
- cure digestion
- eliminate gas
- cure anxiety
- treat insomnia
- stop hair loss
- grow hair fast
- reverse aging
- boost immunity guaranteed
- detox your body from disease
- clinically proven, unless verified with source
- FDA approved supplement

---

## Preferred Safe Language

Use:
- may support
- helps support
- designed to support
- designed to complement
- helps maintain
- supports daily wellness
- supports digestive balance
- supports digestive comfort
- supports gut balance
- supports a calm routine
- supports beauty from within
- supports overall wellness
- part of a consistent self-care routine
- part of a daily wellness ritual
- results may vary
- not medical advice

---

## Customer Pain vs Product Promise

Customer language can describe a lived experience.

Allowed as customer pain:
- I eat and my stomach feels like a balloon.
- I feel heavy after meals.
- My routine feels unpredictable.
- I want something simple.
- I feel like I have tried everything.

Not allowed as product promise:
- Inner Bloom stops bloating.
- Inner Bloom fixes digestion.
- Inner Bloom heals your gut.
- Inner Bloom prevents discomfort.
- Inner Bloom guarantees comfort after meals.

Safe product promise:
- Inner Bloom is designed to support daily digestive wellness.
- Inner Bloom helps support gut balance.
- Inner Bloom is part of a consistent self-care routine.
- Inner Bloom supports overall wellness.

---

## Disclaimer Rules

Use short disclaimer for social captions when mentioning supplement benefits:

Wellness support only. Not medical advice. Results may vary.

Use full disclaimer for product education, PDP copy, email footer, or detailed supplement benefit content:

These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

---

## Approval Checklist

Before content is approved, check:

- No disease claims.
- No hard promises.
- No cure, treat, prevent, diagnose language.
- No shame-based body language.
- No guaranteed outcome.
- Product is positioned as support only.
- Disclaimer is added when supplement benefits are mentioned.
- Human approval is required before publishing.
