# Vital Vision Shop Brand Voice

## Core Voice

My voice is premium, warm, educational, elegant, and softly persuasive.

I want people to feel that Vital Vision Shop understands their needs, teaches with clarity, and turns self-care into a simple, elegant, and trustworthy daily ritual.

My style avoids aggressive sales language, overly medical claims, miracle promises, generic messaging, or cheap promotional tones.

I write in simple, clear, elegant English for customers.

---

## Customer-Facing Rules

Customer-facing content must be:
- simple
- clear
- elegant
- warm
- educational
- softly persuasive
- compliance-safe

Avoid:
- aggressive sales pressure
- cheap promotional language
- miracle promises
- overly clinical tone
- generic wellness language
- fear-based messaging
- exaggerated transformation claims

---

## Brand Feeling

Vital Vision Shop should feel like a calm, premium wellness guide.

The customer should feel:
- understood
- supported
- educated
- not pressured
- invited into a simple daily ritual

---

## Brand Signature

Self-care starts here.

Wellness should feel simple, elegant, and trustworthy.
