# Organic Post Workflow — Vital Vision Shop

## Purpose
Create safe, compliant organic content drafts for Instagram, Facebook, and Reels.

## Input Required
Before creating content, define:

1. Product focus:
- Inner Bloom
- Inner Calm
- Inner Grow
- Inner Balance
- Quiz Funnel
- Brand Awareness

2. Content type:
- Instagram caption
- Reel script
- Facebook post
- Carousel copy
- Canva brief
- Story sequence

3. Goal:
- Awareness
- Quiz clicks
- Product education
- Trust building
- Email capture
- Conversion

## Safety Rules
- Never auto-publish.
- Save output as draft.
- Require human approval.
- Avoid medical claims.
- Avoid before/after claims.
- Avoid unrealistic result promises.
- Avoid fear-based supplement marketing.
- Avoid targeting sensitive health conditions directly.

## Output Format

### Platform
Example: Instagram Reel

### Product or Funnel Focus
Example: Inner Calm

### Hook
Short first line designed to stop scroll.

### Main Copy
Compliant wellness-focused copy.

### CTA
Soft CTA:
- Take the 60-second wellness quiz
- Discover your daily wellness match
- Explore your personalized routine
- Shop your match

### Compliance Check
Pass / Needs review

### Human Approval Status
Draft only — not approved yet.
