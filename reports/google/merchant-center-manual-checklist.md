## SECTION 8 — Misrepresentation Checks

**Menu path:** Merchant Center → Diagnostics → Item issues (filter: Misrepresentation)

Misrepresentation is a critical policy area for supplement/wellness products. Google may flag:

| Risk | What to Check | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Unrealistic claims | Any "guaranteed results" language in titles or descriptions | Remove all guarantee language |
| Before/after imagery | Product images showing body transformation | Do not use |
| False urgency | "Last chance" or countdown timers that reset | Remove if not genuinely limited |
| Price misrepresentation | Feed price differs from live site price | Sync Shopify → MC |
| Unclear business identity | No About page, no contact info | Ensure vitalvision.shop has both |
| Unverifiable claims | "Clinically proven" without study citation | Remove all such claims |

## SECTION 9 — Healthcare / Restricted Pharmaceuticals

**Menu path:** Merchant Center → Diagnostics → filter by "Healthcare and medicines"

Supplements occupy a regulated gray area in Google's policy. Key checks:

| Check | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Products listed as "dietary supplements" | This is the correct category — not "drugs" or "pharmaceuticals" |
| No drug interaction claims | Do not say a product interacts with medications |
| No disease treatment claims | "Supports gut health" is OK; "treats IBS" is not |
| Country targeting | Healthcare ad policies vary by country — confirm US targeting only if not approved for other markets |
| Personalized advertising eligibility | Health products may be restricted from personalized ads — confirm in MC settings |
| Google Health policy — Unapproved pharmaceuticals | Supplements that make drug-like claims may be classified as unapproved pharmaceuticals |
| Shopping ads vs Free listings | If Shopping ads are restricted, Free listings may still be available |

**Required language on all product listings:**
- "Results may vary"
- No cure/treat/prevent/diagnose/reverse/heal/guaranteed language
- No references to specific medical conditions (anxiety, depression, insomnia, IBS, etc.)
