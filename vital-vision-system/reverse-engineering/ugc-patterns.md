# UGC Patterns

## Purpose
Track UGC-style content patterns for founder videos, AI UGC, and talking-to-camera scripts.

## What to Collect
- Hook
- Scene style
- Voiceover structure
- B-roll idea
- Product mention timing
- CTA
- Risk level

## What Not to Copy
Do not copy creators, faces, voices, testimonials, or personal stories from competitors.

## How to Translate Insights
Create original scripts using Vital Vision approved language and Lucy approval before production.
