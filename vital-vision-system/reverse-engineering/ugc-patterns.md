## Populated UGC Patterns

### UGC-001: Ritual → Inner Balance (Ingredient Transparency)
- **Source Competitor:** Ritual
- **Product:** Inner Balance (daily multivitamin)
- **Funnel Stage:** TOFU (Awareness)
- **Creator Persona:** Curious everyday wellness researcher, early 30s, conversational tone. NOT a medical professional. NOT an influencer. Shares perspective, not results.
- **Hook Delivery Style:** Direct camera address. Casual, slightly conspiratorial tone. Sets up an information gap.
  - Example opening: "Nobody talks about what's actually IN a daily multivitamin. Let me break it down."
- **Body Segment Structure (0–30s):**
  - [0–3s] Hook on screen text + talking head to camera. Creates information gap.
  - [3–14s] Educational segment: one clear concept about ingredients or quality. Research voice only. "Studies suggest…" / "What I found when I looked into this…"
  - [14–24s] Product context (soft): "Inner Balance was designed with this in mind…" — educational mention, not a promise.
  - [24–28s] CTA: single action. "Link in bio if you want to dig into the details."
- **Product Mention Timing:** After educational segment — never before establishing educational credibility.
- **Product Framing:** Educational curiosity ("designed to support") — not personal testimonial.
- **CTA Style:** Low friction. Research framing. "Link in bio" or "Details in bio."
- **Compliance Anchors:** No personal health results. No before/after framing. No IBS/disease terms. Research voice only.
- **What Works:** Creates information gap → positions creator as helpful researcher → product mention feels like a natural next step, not an ad.

### UGC-002: Seed → Inner Bloom (Microbiome Curiosity)
- **Source Competitor:** Seed
- **Product:** Inner Bloom (probiotic)
- **Funnel Stage:** TOFU (Awareness)
- **Creator Persona:** Wellness-curious everyday person, late 20s–early 30s. Research-framing voice. Warm and conversational. NOT a probiotic expert — someone who went and looked it up.
- **Hook Delivery Style:** First-person curiosity journey. "I had no idea what [concept] meant, so I started researching." Relatable starting point of genuine ignorance → discovery.
  - Example opening: "I had no idea what 'gut support' actually meant — so I started researching. Here's what I found."
- **Body Segment Structure (0–30s):**
  - [0–3s] Hook: first-person curiosity frame. Talking head. On screen text reinforces.
  - [3–14s] Educational arc: what they discovered about gut wellness / probiotic support. Research voice. No claims, pure curiosity.
  - [14–24s] Product context (soft): "Inner Bloom is designed to support your daily gut wellness routine." Mention feels natural after education.
  - [24–28s] CTA: "Link in bio if you want to dig into the details."
- **Product Mention Timing:** After the research reveal — product is positioned as the logical continuation of curiosity, not the point of the video.
- **Product Framing:** Research extension ("designed to support") — not testimonial.
- **CTA Style:** Curiosity-led. "Dig into the details." "Find out more." Low pressure.
- **Compliance Anchors:** No IBS/leaky gut/disease terms. No personal gut health outcomes. Research framing only. No before/after.
- **What Works:** First-person curiosity journey avoids "ad" response. Educational reveal structure drives view-through rate. Non-testimonial compliance by design.

### UGC-003: Love Wellness → Inner Bloom + Inner Calm (Ritual Framing)
- **Source Competitor:** Love Wellness
- **Product:** Inner Bloom + Inner Calm (probiotic + magnesium pair)
- **Funnel Stage:** MOFU (Consideration)
- **Creator Persona:** Relatable everyday wellness woman, late 20s–mid 30s. Intentional living aesthetic. Warm and normalizing. NOT a wellness perfectionist — someone building their own ritual with intention.
- **Hook Delivery Style:** Ritual identity hook. Sets a personal standard, invites reflection. "My morning ritual has one rule." Triggers identity alignment and curiosity about the rule.
  - Example opening: "My morning ritual has one rule: I only keep things that actually belong in my routine."
- **Body Segment Structure (0–30s):**
  - [0–3s] Hook: personal ritual standard. Talking head or B-roll of morning setup.
  - [3–14s] Ritual context: why intentionality in a routine matters. Permission-giving tone. "You don't need to do everything — just what belongs."
  - [14–24s] Product moment: both Inner Bloom and Inner Calm introduced as ritual elements. "These belong in mine." Preference framing, not testimonial.
  - [24–28s] CTA: "If you're exploring what belongs in your routine, link in bio is worth a look."
- **Product Mention Timing:** Mid-video — after ritual philosophy is established. Product is positioned as ritual tool, not cure.
- **Product Framing:** Ritual preference framing ("that's what I look for") — NOT a health testimonial, NOT a result.
- **CTA Style:** Soft invitation. "Worth a look." Community framing. "Tell us below."
- **Compliance Anchors:** NO hormone claims. NO digestive treatment claims. Ritual preference only. Product is a ritual tool, not a fix.
- **What Works:** Identity hook triggers self-reflection. Normalizes intentional (not perfect) self-care. Multi-product mention feels natural in ritual context. High comment engagement ("what's in your routine?").

## UGC Script Template (for new content generation)

Use this structure when writing original VV UGC scripts. Replace all [BRACKETS].

```
[0–3s] Hook (on screen text + talking head):
"[CURIOSITY OR RITUAL HOOK — under 10 words]"

[3–14s] Educational segment:
"[ONE CLEAR CONCEPT. Research voice. No claims. No disease terms.]"

[14–24s] Product context (soft):
"[PRODUCT NAME] is designed to [SAFE STRUCTURE/FUNCTION LANGUAGE]. [OPTIONAL: one differentiator]."

[24–28s] CTA:
"[LOW-FRICTION SINGLE CTA. Link in bio / Comment [KEYWORD] / Save this.]"
```

**Compliance checklist for UGC scripts:**
- [ ] No personal health results claimed
- [ ] No before/after framing
- [ ] No disease names as treatment targets
- [ ] "May support" / "designed to support" language only
- [ ] Creator is a curious researcher, not a medical authority
- [ ] Lucy approval before production

## What NOT to Copy

- Competitor creators' faces, voices, or personal stories
- Competitor-specific script lines or hooks word-for-word
- Any creator testimonials or personal results
- Medical or disease-focused hooks
- Before/after wellness transformation structures
