## Validated Content Formats (Currently in Production)

### FORMAT-001: AI UGC Short-Form Video (9:16)
- **Platforms:** Instagram Reels + Facebook Reels (cross-post)
- **Duration:** 24–30 seconds
- **Funnel Stage:** TOFU (Awareness) / MOFU (Consideration)
- **Structure:**
  - [0–3s] Hook — on screen text + creator talking head. Stops the scroll.
  - [3–14s] Educational segment — one clear concept. No claims. Research or curiosity voice.
  - [14–24s] Product context — soft educational mention. "designed to support," never a promise.
  - [24–28s] CTA — single action. "Link in bio" or "Comment [KEYWORD]."
- **Creator Style:** Curious everyday person (late 20s–mid 30s). NOT an influencer. NOT a medical professional. Research voice only.
- **Compliance Anchors:** No fake testimonials. No personal health results. Creator shares perspective, not results.
- **Source Competitors:** Ritual (ingredient transparency), Seed (microbiome curiosity), Love Wellness (ritual framing)
- **Metric To Watch:** View-through rate (VTR), Saves, Profile visits from Reel
- **VV OCQ Rows:** rows 6 (Seed/Inner Bloom UGC), 8 (Love Wellness/Inner Bloom+Calm UGC)

### FORMAT-002: Educational Carousel (4:5, 1080x1080px per slide)
- **Platforms:** Instagram Feed + Facebook Feed
- **Slides:** 5 slides (cover + 3 educational + CTA)
- **Funnel Stage:** TOFU (Awareness) / MOFU (Consideration)
- **Structure:**
  - Slide 1 (Cover): Hook headline. Creates the scroll. Product barely visible or not at all.
  - Slide 2: First educational concept. One idea per slide. Simple.
  - Slide 3: Second educational concept.
  - Slide 4: Third educational concept or transparency/values moment.
  - Slide 5 (CTA): Product soft mention + CTA + disclaimer + brand signature.
- **Design System:** Text-card based (no real photography required). Botanical line art. Serif headline + clean sans body. Warm neutral palette.
- **Compliance Anchors:** No treatment claims on any slide. "Results may vary" on final slide if benefit mentioned. FDA disclaimer if applicable.
- **Source Competitors:** Ritual (ingredient transparency carousels), Seed (microbiome literacy carousels), Love Wellness (ritual simplification carousels)
- **Metric To Watch:** Carousel completion rate, Saves, Comment quality (educational engagement)
- **VV OCQ Rows:** rows 6 (Seed/Inner Bloom carousel), 9 (Love Wellness/Inner Bloom+Calm carousel)

### FORMAT-003: Story CTA Asset (9:16, 1080x1920px)
- **Platforms:** Instagram Stories + Facebook Stories
- **Format:** Single static frame (designed in Canva)
- **Funnel Stage:** BOFU (Conversion)
- **Structure:**
  - Full-bleed branded background (sage, blush, peach, or lavender)
  - Product bottle(s) centered — subtle drop shadow
  - Compact serif headline at top (curiosity or soft invitation hook)
  - 2-line body copy mid-frame (designed to support + one differentiator)
  - Link sticker at bottom ("Tap to explore →")
  - Brand logo bottom corner
- **Compliance Anchors:** No benefit claims as text on the story frame. Product is the hero. Link destination must also be compliance-reviewed.
- **Source Competitors:** Ritual (product discovery CTAs), Seed (curiosity-based story taps), Love Wellness (ritual invitation story frames)
- **Metric To Watch:** Story tap-through rate (TTR), Link clicks
- **VV OCQ Rows:** rows 7 (Seed/Inner Bloom story), 10 (Love Wellness/Inner Bloom+Calm story)
