### HOOK-001
- **Source Competitor:** Ritual

- **Funnel Stage:** TOFU (Awareness)
- **Pattern Type:** Curiosity / Ingredient transparency

## Hook Pattern Templates (for new content generation)

Use these templates when generating new hooks. Replace placeholders.

### Curiosity / Ingredient transparency
"Nobody talks about what's actually in [PRODUCT CATEGORY]. Let me break it down."
→ Triggered by: ingredient confusion, supplement skepticism

### Research reveal
"I had no idea what '[CONCEPT]' actually meant — so I started researching. Here's what I found."
→ Triggered by: education gap, research curiosity

### Provocative open question
"The [TOPIC] question no one is asking — but everyone should be."
→ Triggered by: curiosity gap, carousel save behavior

### Ritual framing
"My [MORNING/EVENING] ritual has one rule: [SIMPLE QUALIFYING STATEMENT]."
→ Triggered by: identity alignment, intentional self-care

### Permission-giving
"Your [ROUTINE TYPE] doesn't need to be complicated."
→ Triggered by: wellness overwhelm, relief

### Curiosity + soft CTA
"Curious about [PRODUCT BENEFIT AREA] for your daily routine?"
→ Triggered by: low-friction consideration, BOFU tap

## What NOT to Copy

- Competitor's exact hook wording
- Competitor's brand-specific language
- Any testimonial or results-based framing from competitor content
- Medical or disease-specific hooks
