# Competitor List

## Purpose
Track wellness, supplement, UGC, Canva, and content references for Vital Vision Shop.

## What to Collect
- Brand name
- Instagram URL
- Platform
- Category or angle
- Why study this brand
- Product match
- Notes about positioning

## What Not to Copy
- Exact captions
- Exact designs
- Exact testimonials
- Product claims
- Medical claims

## How to Translate Insights
Use patterns only. Convert them into original Vital Vision content using the brand voice and compliance framework.

## Compliance Notes
Avoid disease, cure, treatment, prevention, before and after, guaranteed results, or fear-based claims.
