## Populated Canva Card Patterns

### CARD-001: Ritual → Inner Balance Carousel (Ingredient Transparency)
- **Source Competitor:** Ritual
- **Product:** Inner Balance
- **Format:** Educational Carousel — 5 slides, 1080x1080px per slide (4:5 for feed)
- **Funnel Stage:** TOFU (Awareness)
- **Visual Mood:** Clean, text-forward. Scientific but approachable. Ingredient-led. Warm neutrals with selective color accent. Premium, uncluttered.
- **Slide Structure:**
  - **Slide 1 (Cover):** Bold hook headline. Ingredient or quality curiosity angle. Product barely visible or absent. Hook creates reason to swipe.
    - Text: Large headline + optional sub-line. No body copy.
    - Visual: Brand color block background or minimal botanical accent. No product photography required.
  - **Slides 2–4 (Educational):** One concept per slide. No more than 2-3 short lines per slide. Icon or simple visual accent. White or off-white background.
    - Text: Small section header + 1–2 lines of educational body.
    - Visual: Botanical line art or subtle texture. No clinical imagery.
  - **Slide 5 (CTA):** Soft product mention + single CTA + disclaimer + brand signature.
    - Text: Product name. One CTA line. "Results may vary." FDA disclaimer if benefit claimed.
    - Visual: Product bottle visible (lifestyle flat lay direction). Logo bottom right.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Hook (largest) → Section label (medium, brand green) → Educational body (small, light weight)
- **CTA Placement:** Slide 5 only. "Explore Inner Balance → link in bio." One CTA, not repeated on earlier slides.
- **Compliance Notes:** No treatment claims on ANY slide. "Results may vary" on final slide if benefit mentioned. FDA disclaimer on final slide if benefit stated.
- **VV Color Application:** Cover: brand green (#1F3D2B) background with white text. Body slides: off-white (#F9F8F6). Accents: soft sage (#E8EDE6).
- **What Works:** Text-forward carousel = no photography required, fast to produce. Swipe-driver on cover. Saves driven by educational value.

### CARD-002: Seed → Inner Bloom Carousel (Microbiome Literacy)
- **Source Competitor:** Seed
- **Product:** Inner Bloom
- **Format:** Educational Carousel — 5 slides, 1080x1080px per slide
- **Funnel Stage:** TOFU (Awareness)
- **Visual Mood:** Science-meets-warmth. Curious. Visually interesting without being clinical. Botanical accents or microbiome-adjacent imagery (organic, not medical).
- **Slide Structure:**
  - **Slide 1 (Cover):** Provocative open question. Curiosity-gap mechanic. "The gut wellness question no one is asking — but everyone should be." Large type. Minimal design.
  - **Slides 2–4 (Educational):** Microbiome literacy concepts. One clear insight per slide. "Did you know…" or "Here's what the research suggests:" framing. No outcome claims.
  - **Slide 5 (CTA):** Inner Bloom soft mention + curiosity CTA + disclaimer + logo.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Cover question (bold, full-bleed style) → Body slides: labeled header + short educational paragraph
- **CTA Placement:** Final slide + optional "swipe to find out" micro-text on cover slide (not a CTA, just a swipe prompt).
- **Compliance Notes:** No IBS/leaky gut/disease language on any slide. Educational framing only. "Results may vary" if any benefit appears. FDA disclaimer on final slide.
- **VV Color Application:** Cover: contrasting text on off-white or sage. Body slides: white with brand green accents. Final slide: product photography direction (flat lay, warm surface).
- **What Works:** Provocative cover drives high save-rates. Educational body positions brand as knowledge leader. Carousel completion rate metric to watch.

### CARD-003: Love Wellness → Inner Bloom + Inner Calm Story CTA (Ritual Invitation)
- **Source Competitor:** Love Wellness
- **Product:** Inner Bloom + Inner Calm
- **Format:** Story CTA Asset — single static frame, 1080x1920px (9:16)
- **Funnel Stage:** BOFU (Conversion)
- **Visual Mood:** Warm invitation. Ritual-positive. Aspirational but approachable. Full-bleed color background. Product as hero.
- **Frame Structure (single slide):**
  - **Background:** Full-bleed brand color (sage, blush, peach, or lavender — VV adaptation: soft sage #E8EDE6 or off-white #F9F8F6)
  - **Product placement:** Bottle(s) centered or lower center. Subtle drop shadow. Product is the visual hero.
  - **Headline (top):** Compact serif-adjacent or bold sans headline. Curiosity or soft ritual invitation hook. Max 6 words.
    - Example: "Your daily wellness ritual starts here."
  - **Body copy (mid-frame):** 2 lines. Designed to support + one differentiator. "Designed to support your daily routine. Formulated for everyday wellness."
  - **Link sticker position (bottom):** "Tap to explore →" — link sticker area. Logo bottom corner.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Headline (large, bold) → Body copy (medium, light) → Link sticker prompt (small)
- **CTA Placement:** Bottom. Link sticker. "Tap to explore →" — no other CTAs.
- **Compliance Notes:** No benefit claims as text on the story frame. Product is the visual, not the claim. Link destination must also be compliance-reviewed.
- **VV Color Application:** Primary background: soft sage (#E8EDE6) or off-white (#F9F8F6). Headline: brand green (#1F3D2B). Logo: brand green.
- **What Works:** Low-friction BOFU tap. Curiosity-to-click without pressure. Clean visual = fast to produce. Story tap-through rate (TTR) is the primary metric.

## VV Canva Card Design Invariants

Apply to ALL Vital Vision Canva cards regardless of format:
- Colors: #1F3D2B (brand green), #F9F8F6 (off-white), #E8EDE6 (soft sage), #FFFFFF (white)
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans / DM Sans / Inter — no script fonts, no decorative fonts
- Photography: soft natural light, neutral surfaces, no clinical or medical imagery, no before/after
- Logo: bottom center or bottom right, minimum 20px clear space
- Compliance: "Results may vary." on final slide/frame if any benefit is stated
- FDA disclaimer on any slide/frame where a structure/function claim appears

## What NOT to Copy

- Exact card layouts, slide counts, or design templates from competitor brands
- Competitor product images or photography
- Competitor testimonials or real customer quotes
- Any before/after or results-specific visual structure
- Competitor font systems or color palettes
