# Canva Card Patterns

## Purpose
Store safe visual/content patterns for Canva cards, carousels, story cards, and feed graphics.

## What to Collect
- Card type
- Slide structure
- Text hierarchy
- CTA placement
- Visual mood
- Compliance notes

## What Not to Copy
Do not copy exact designs, layouts, fonts, product images, or competitor brand systems.

## How to Translate Insights
Create original Vital Vision card briefs using approved colors, brand voice, and compliance rules.
