# Comment Insights

## Purpose
Capture real audience language and recurring pain points from comments and community research.

## What to Collect
- Repeated phrases
- Objections
- Confusions
- Desired outcomes
- Emotional language
- Questions people ask

## What Not to Copy
Do not quote private users directly unless anonymized and transformed.

## How to Translate Insights
Turn repeated language into original hooks, FAQs, ManyChat replies, and educational content.
