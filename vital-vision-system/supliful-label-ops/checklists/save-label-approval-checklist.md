## Review Completion

- [ ] Workflow 01 (Review Migrated Label) has been completed for this label, OR Workflow 02 (Create New Label) has been completed
- [ ] All four specialist agents have reviewed the label (Label Architect, Compliance Reviewer, Brand Guardian, Design Critic)
- [ ] QA Label Reviewer has issued a final decision
- [ ] QA decision is: **SAVE** (if the decision is REVISE or ESCALATE, stop — do not complete this checklist)

---

## Compliance Confirmation

- [ ] No forbidden language is present on the label (no "cures," "treats," "prevents disease," "heals," "guarantees," "diagnoses")
- [ ] FDA disclaimer is present and reads correctly: "These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- [ ] Supplement Facts panel is present and complete
- [ ] Suggested Use is present
- [ ] Caution / Warning is present
- [ ] Manufacturer or distributor name and address is present
- [ ] Net quantity is stated

---

## Layout Confirmation

- [ ] All critical text is within the safe area (nothing at risk of being cut off)
- [ ] Matrix code / barcode zone is clear
- [ ] No duplicated or stacked elements from a previous label version

---

## Brand Confirmation

- [ ] Product name is spelled correctly
- [ ] Subtitle matches the approved version
- [ ] Label looks premium and brand-consistent

---

## Final Visual Scan (Do This On Screen Right Now)
