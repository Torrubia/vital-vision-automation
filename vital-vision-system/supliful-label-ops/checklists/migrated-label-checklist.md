# Migrated Label Checklist

Use this checklist while looking at the migrated label in the Supliful editor on screen.
Do not check any item from memory. Confirm each one visually.

**Product:** ___________________________
**Date:** ___________________________
**Reviewer:** ___________________________

---

## Section 1 — Layout and Structure

- [ ] Label is displaying in the correct orientation (not rotated or upside down)
- [ ] Front panel and back panel are clearly separated
- [ ] Product name is visible on the front panel
- [ ] Key benefit text is visible on the front panel
- [ ] Supplement Facts table is visible on the back panel
- [ ] All text appears to be within the safe area (not cut off at edges)
- [ ] The bleed area does not contain critical text or logos
- [ ] The matrix code / barcode zone is clear and unobstructed
- [ ] No text blocks appear duplicated or stacked on top of each other
- [ ] No elements from the previous label version appear to be floating or misplaced

---

## Section 2 — Required Label Elements

- [ ] Product name is present and correct: ___________________________
- [ ] Supplement Facts panel is present
- [ ] Serving size is stated in Supplement Facts
- [ ] Servings per container is stated in Supplement Facts
- [ ] Each ingredient is listed with its amount
- [ ] % Daily Value column is present (or † where no DV established)
- [ ] Suggested Use section is present
- [ ] Caution or Warning section is present
- [ ] FDA disclaimer is present
- [ ] Manufacturer or distributor name is present
- [ ] Manufacturer or distributor address is present
- [ ] Net quantity of contents is stated (e.g., "60 Capsules")

---

## Section 3 — Compliance Language

- [ ] No claim says "cures" anything
- [ ] No claim says "treats" a disease or condition
- [ ] No claim says "prevents" a disease
- [ ] No claim says "heals" anything
- [ ] No claim says "guarantees" results
- [ ] No claim says "diagnoses" anything
- [ ] All benefit statements use safe language ("supports," "helps support," "may help support," "helps maintain")
- [ ] FDA disclaimer is complete: "These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."

---

## Section 4 — Brand Consistency

- [ ] Product name spelling is correct: Inner Balance / Inner Bloom / Inner Calm / Inner Grow (circle one)
- [ ] Product subtitle matches the approved version
- [ ] Vital Vision brand name appears correctly
- [ ] No competitor names appear
- [ ] Tone feels premium and wellness-focused (not aggressive or hype-based)
- [ ] No temporary promotional copy appears (no pricing, no "limited time" offers)

---

## Section 5 — Legibility

- [ ] Product name is readable at a glance
- [ ] Body text (Suggested Use, Caution, address) appears legible
- [ ] Supplement Facts text appears legible
- [ ] Text contrast is sufficient against background
- [ ] No ingredient names are cut off in the Supplement Facts table

---
