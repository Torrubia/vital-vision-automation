## Product Identity

- [ ] Product name is spelled correctly
  - Inner Bloom — Advanced Probiotic Formula
  - Inner Calm — Magnesium Glycinate
  - Inner Balance — Complete Multivitamin
  - Inner Grow — Hair, Skin & Nails Support
- [ ] Product subtitle matches the approved version (see above)
- [ ] Product name on the label matches the product name in Supliful and in Shopify
- [ ] Net quantity statement matches the Shopify variant (e.g., "60 Capsules")

---

## Brand Presence

- [ ] "Vital Vision" brand name appears on the label
- [ ] Brand name is spelled correctly ("Vital Vision" — not "Vital-Vision," "VitalVision," or "Vital Vison")
- [ ] Logo is the correct current version
- [ ] Logo placement follows the brand visual guidelines
- [ ] Logo is not distorted, pixelated, or stretched

---

## Visual Identity

- [ ] Brand color palette is consistent with approved Vital Vision colors
- [ ] Typography feels consistent with the Vital Vision premium aesthetic
- [ ] No generic or cheap-looking design elements that conflict with the brand
- [ ] Label has a clean, premium appearance that matches the brand positioning
- [ ] If lifestyle imagery is used, it is consistent with Vital Vision's warm, wellness aesthetic

---

## Brand Tone and Language

- [ ] Label text sounds premium and wellness-focused (not aggressive or hype-based)
- [ ] No generic supplement language that sounds white-label ("MEGA DOSE," "SUPER STRENGTH," "EXTREME FORMULA")
- [ ] Key benefit language is consistent with the approved product angle for this product:
  - Inner Bloom: digestive balance, gut microbiome support, daily wellness
  - Inner Calm: magnesium for calm, relaxation support, sleep quality support
  - Inner Balance: complete daily nutrition, multivitamin support, overall wellness
  - Inner Grow: hair, skin, and nails support, beauty from within
- [ ] Tone is warm, elegant, and educational — not fear-based or salesy

---

## What Should Not Be on the Label

- [ ] No competitor brand names or references
- [ ] No pricing ("$29.99," "Sale," "50% Off")
- [ ] No temporary promotional text ("Limited time offer," "Buy 2 Get 1 Free")
- [ ] No social media handles (unless intentionally designed into a permanent label element)
- [ ] No website URL that might change (unless intentional)
