## Forbidden Language — These must NOT appear anywhere on the label

- [ ] "cures" — NOT present
- [ ] "treats" (as in treating a disease) — NOT present
- [ ] "prevents" (as in preventing a disease) — NOT present
- [ ] "heals" — NOT present
- [ ] "reverses" — NOT present
- [ ] "eliminates" (as a medical claim) — NOT present
- [ ] "fixes" (as a medical claim) — NOT present
- [ ] "diagnoses" — NOT present
- [ ] "guaranteed results" or "guarantees" — NOT present
- [ ] "clinically proven to [cure/treat/prevent]" — NOT present
- [ ] "as effective as medication" — NOT present
- [ ] "replaces medication" — NOT present
- [ ] "FDA approved" (supplements cannot use this) — NOT present
- [ ] Disease name used as a claim target (e.g., "for diabetes," "for depression," "for cancer") — NOT present
- [ ] "anxiety treatment" or "treats anxiety" — NOT present
- [ ] "hormone cure" — NOT present
- [ ] "gut healing guarantee" — NOT present
- [ ] "weight loss guaranteed" — NOT present

---

## Approved Language — These are acceptable on the label

- [ ] "supports" — acceptable when used (not required)
- [ ] "helps support" — acceptable
- [ ] "may help support" — acceptable
- [ ] "helps maintain" — acceptable
- [ ] "designed to complement" — acceptable
- [ ] "part of a balanced wellness routine" — acceptable
- [ ] "crafted for daily use" — acceptable
- [ ] Ingredient-level benefit claims that are structure/function only — acceptable

---

## Required FDA Disclaimer

- [ ] FDA disclaimer is present on the label
- [ ] FDA disclaimer reads exactly: "These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- [ ] FDA disclaimer text is legible (not too small to read)
- [ ] FDA disclaimer is near any structure/function claim or in a clearly visible location

---

## Structure/Function Claim Rules

If any claim on the label describes what the product does for the body's structure or function:

- [ ] The claim is truthful and not misleading
- [ ] The claim is about the body's structure or function, not about a disease
- [ ] The claim does not use disease names as the target condition
- [ ] The FDA disclaimer accompanies the claim

---

## Suggested Use Language

- [ ] Suggested Use uses instructional language ("Take 2 capsules daily with water")
- [ ] Suggested Use does not include disease-treatment language
- [ ] Suggested Use does not promise specific outcomes

---

## Caution / Warning Language

- [ ] Caution text is present
- [ ] Caution advises consulting a healthcare professional if relevant (e.g., if pregnant, nursing, have a medical condition, or taking medications)
- [ ] Caution advises keeping out of reach of children (required)
- [ ] No Caution language creates an implied disease-treatment claim
