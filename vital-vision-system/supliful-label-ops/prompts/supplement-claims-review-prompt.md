## Review Rules

### Forbidden — Flag and Reject

Any of the following make a claim non-compliant. Flag them immediately:

- Words that imply treatment of a disease: "treats," "cures," "heals," "reverses," "eliminates" (when used as a medical outcome)
- Words that imply disease prevention: "prevents [disease]," "protects against [disease name]"
- Disease names used as the benefit target: "for diabetes," "for depression," "for cancer," "for anxiety disorder," "for hypertension"
- Guaranteed outcome language: "guaranteed results," "will," "you will feel," "proven to work"
- Implied medication comparison: "as effective as medication," "replaces your doctor's advice"

### Approved — These Are Fine

- "supports," "helps support," "may help support"
- "helps maintain"
- "designed to complement a daily wellness routine"
- "crafted for daily use"
- "part of a balanced self-care ritual"
- Ingredient-level structure/function claims (e.g., "Magnesium supports muscle function")
- Educational statements about the ingredient without a specific disease claim
