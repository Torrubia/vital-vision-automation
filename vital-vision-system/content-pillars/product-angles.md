
# Inner Bloom — Hook Multiplier

## Product
Inner Bloom — Advanced Probiotic Formula

## Use Case
Hooks for organic content, email subject lines, Reels, Story first lines, landing page headlines, and soft-sell wellness education.

## Compliance Rules
- Do not claim to cure, treat, diagnose, prevent, or reverse disease.
- Avoid hard promises like “no more bloating,” “fix your gut,” or “guaranteed results.”
- Use soft wellness language such as “may support,” “helps support,” “daily digestive wellness,” “gut balance,” and “digestive comfort.”
- Keep tone premium, calm, intelligent, and emotionally specific.

## Category 1: Curiosity Hooks

1. Why your stomach feels different after the same meals.
2. The gentle routine your digestion may have been asking for.
3. What changes when gut support becomes a daily habit.
4. The overlooked reason your meals feel harder lately.
5. A calmer way to think about daily digestive comfort.

## Category 2: Contrarian Hooks

1. Your digestion may not need another extreme reset.
2. Feeling better after meals is not about eating perfectly.
3. A harsh cleanse is not a daily wellness routine.
4. Your gut routine may need consistency, not restriction.
5. More rules may not be the answer for digestive comfort.

## Category 3: Specific and Numbered Hooks

1. One daily ritual for gentler digestive wellness support.
2. Three signs your gut routine needs more consistency.
3. The five minute habit that supports digestive balance.
4. One bottle, one routine, one calmer wellness rhythm.
5. Seven days of softer support for daily digestive wellness.

## Category 4: Identity and Transformation Hooks

1. Become the person who feels calmer around food again.
2. For the woman rebuilding trust with her daily wellness routine.
3. A softer routine for people tired of digestive guesswork.
4. Feel less ruled by meals, more connected to yourself.
5. Your self-care routine can start with gut balance.

## Top 3 Winners

1. Feeling better after meals is not about eating perfectly.  
Why it wins: It challenges a common belief while speaking directly to frustration, guilt, and the desire for a more realistic wellness routine.

2. Become the person who feels calmer around food again.  
Why it wins: It is emotionally specific and identity-driven, making the reader feel seen without making a medical or guaranteed-result claim.

3. Your gut routine may need consistency, not restriction.  
Why it wins: It positions Inner Bloom around daily ritual and balance while creating a strong contrarian angle against extreme diets and cleanses.
