# Vital Vision Shop — Visual Rules

## Brand Feel
Premium, calm, warm, elegant, educational, and softly persuasive.

## Core Visual Direction
- Natural wellness aesthetic
- Soft premium backgrounds
- Clean product-focused layouts
- Calm lifestyle imagery
- Elegant spacing
- Minimal clutter
- High readability on mobile

## Color Direction
Primary:
- Deep wellness green
- Warm neutral backgrounds
- Soft gold accents

Product mood:
- Inner Bloom: digestive wellness, fresh, balanced, clean
- Inner Calm: calm, evening, soothing, soft
- Inner Balance: daily foundation, steady, complete
- Inner Grow: beauty wellness, soft feminine premium

## Typography Direction
- Clean sans-serif for readability
- Elegant serif only for premium accents
- Avoid crowded text
- Prioritize mobile-first hierarchy

## Layout Rules
- One main message per visual
- Strong headline
- Short supporting line
- Clear CTA or save/share cue
- Keep product visible when product-led
- Avoid overloading with claims

## Do Not Use
- Medical imagery
- Hospital/clinical visuals
- Harsh before/after imagery
- Cheap sale graphics
- Overly loud colors
- Fear-based visual framing
