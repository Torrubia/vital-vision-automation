Vital Vision Shop offers premium daily wellness support designed to help customers build simple, elegant, consistent self-care rituals.

## Current Offer Types
- Daily wellness support
- Product-based self-care routines
- Educational product recommendations
- Soft quiz-based product discovery
- Organic content-driven product education

## Current Soft Offer Language
Use:
- Discover your daily wellness match
- Build a simple self-care ritual
- Support your everyday wellness routine
- Find the support that fits your current self-care need
- Make wellness feel simple, calm, and consistent

Avoid:
- Miracle results
- Disease treatment promises
- Aggressive discounts as the main selling point
- Medical or pharmaceutical positioning
- Fear-based urgency
