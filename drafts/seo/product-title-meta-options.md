## Format Notes

- Google truncates titles at ~60 chars and meta descriptions at ~155 chars
- Product titles in Shopping feed are separate from SEO page titles
- Pattern: [Product Name] — [Key Benefit] | [Brand Name]
- Include "supplement" keyword in title for Google Shopping category matching
- Include "daily" or "routine" for semantic wellness context
