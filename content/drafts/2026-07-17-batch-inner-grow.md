## CUSTOMER LANGUAGE FOUNDATION

### Top Customer Pain
She invests in beauty routines — serums, masks, treatments — but her routine still feels incomplete. She is doing the outside work but missing internal support.

### Emotional Consequence
She feels frustrated that she spends time and money on beauty but cannot find consistency. She worries she is missing something. She wants a beauty ritual that feels whole and simple, not expensive and scattered.

### What She Has Tried
Serums, hair masks, nail treatments, collagen drinks, expensive skincare, salon treatments, viral beauty routines, random beauty supplements, makeup to cover rather than support. She starts new routines and stops when they become too complicated or expensive to maintain.

### Why Those Solutions Failed
Most solutions focus only on the outside, require a lot of products, or are too complex to keep up. She wants a daily step that feels simple, consistent, and nourishing from within.

### Hidden Objection
She does not want to waste money on another beauty product she will not stay consistent with.

### Customer Language Bank
1. I use so many beauty products, but my routine still feels incomplete.
2. I want beauty support from within.
3. I want my hair, skin, and nails routine to feel simple.
4. I do not want another complicated beauty routine.
5. I want consistency, not another random product.
6. I want to feel polished and supported daily.
7. I am tired of buying another serum and still feeling inconsistent.
8. I want my beauty routine to start before makeup.
9. I want something easy to add to my morning.
10. I want beauty to feel like self-care, not pressure.
