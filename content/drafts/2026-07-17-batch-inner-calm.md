## CUSTOMER LANGUAGE FOUNDATION

### Top Customer Pain
The day ends, but the body does not. She feels tense, mentally full, and unable to transition into rest — even when nothing is technically wrong.

### Emotional Consequence
She feels frustrated that her evenings are restless despite wanting calm. She scrolls, stays "on," and wakes up feeling like she never fully wound down. She wants a softer end to her day but cannot find a ritual she can actually repeat.

### What She Has Tried
Herbal teas, meditation apps, baths, candles, scrolling less (unsuccessfully), magnesium products she forgot to take consistently, cutting caffeine, trying to sleep earlier without changing her actual routine.

### Why Those Solutions Failed
They required too much willpower or were too complicated to repeat on difficult nights. She needs a simple ritual she can return to — not a perfect routine.

### Hidden Objection
She does not want another supplement or step that becomes one more thing to manage.

### Customer Language Bank
1. My body feels busy even when my day is over.
2. I want to slow down, but I do not know where to start.
3. I scroll at night because my mind still feels full.
4. I want a calmer evening routine that feels easy to repeat.
5. I feel like I am always "on."
6. I need something simple before bed.
7. My self-care only happens when everything else is done.
8. I want to feel more grounded at the end of the day.
9. I do not want a complicated wellness routine at night.
10. I want a small ritual that helps me transition into rest.
