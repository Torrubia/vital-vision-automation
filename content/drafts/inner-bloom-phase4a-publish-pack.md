## CUSTOMER LANGUAGE FOUNDATION

Before all content was written, the following customer language was mapped from config/product-library.md and the customer-language-research skill.

### Top Customer Pain
Eating feels unpredictable. The stomach expands and feels heavy or tight after meals, regardless of how carefully the customer eats.

### Emotional Consequence
She feels self-conscious around meals. She avoids certain foods, events, or outfits. She feels frustrated because she is "doing everything right" and still feels uncomfortable. She wants simplicity and consistency, not another complicated solution.

### What She Has Tried
Eating lighter. Avoiding certain foods. Trying different diets. Cutting things out one by one. She has spent energy trying to identify the problem and has found no consistent answer.

### Hidden Objection
She does not believe another supplement will actually be different. She is skeptical of products that promise results. She wants to feel understood before she considers a product.
