# Inner Calm Organic Product Seed

## Product

Inner Calm

## Category

Magnesium glycinate, calm routine, evening wellness ritual, daily relaxation support, and self-care consistency.

## Core Pain

My body feels busy even when my day is over.

## Audience

People who feel mentally full, emotionally stretched, tense after long days, inconsistent with evening self-care, or frustrated because they want to slow down but do not want a complicated nighttime routine.

They are looking for a simple daily wellness habit that may support relaxation, calm routines, daily balance, and a more consistent evening ritual.

## Audience Language

1. My body feels busy even when my day is over.
2. I want to slow down, but I do not know where to start.
3. I scroll at night because my mind still feels full.
4. I want a calmer evening routine that feels easy to repeat.
5. I feel like I am always “on.”
6. I need something simple before bed.
7. My self-care only happens when everything else is done.
8. I want to feel more grounded at the end of the day.
9. I do not want a complicated wellness routine at night.
10. I want a small ritual that helps me transition into rest.

## What They Have Already Tried

1. Herbal teas
2. Scrolling less before bed
3. Random nighttime routines
4. Meditation apps
5. Magnesium products without consistency
6. Baths, candles, or relaxing playlists
7. Cutting caffeine
8. Trying to sleep earlier without changing the routine

## Why Those Solutions Failed

Most solutions were either too inconsistent, too dependent on willpower, or too hard to repeat on busy nights.

The customer does not need a perfect evening routine. She needs a simple ritual she can return to consistently.

## Hidden Objection

I do not want another supplement or routine that becomes one more thing to manage.

## Organic Angle

A simple evening wellness ritual for people who want to feel more grounded, calm, and consistent at the end of the day.

## Safe Product Bridge

Inner Calm is designed to support a calm routine, relaxation, daily balance, and overall wellness as part of a simple evening self-care ritual.

## Primary CTAs

- Comment CALM for the evening ritual.
- Explore Inner Calm.
- Save this for tonight’s self-care routine.
- Start your calm evening ritual.
- Build a softer end-of-day routine.

## Avoid

- Cure anxiety.
- Treat insomnia.
- Fix stress.
- Guaranteed sleep.
- Calm your nervous system, unless reviewed and supported.
- Stop overthinking.
- Eliminate stress.
- Sleep better overnight.
- Works instantly.
- Medical or mental health claims.
