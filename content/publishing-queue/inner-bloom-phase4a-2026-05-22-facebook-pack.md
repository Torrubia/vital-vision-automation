### HOW TO CROSS-POST REEL TO FACEBOOK

**Option A — Instagram App (during Reel publish)**
1. When publishing the Instagram Reel, toggle on "Share to Facebook" before tapping Share.
2. The Reel posts to both Instagram and your connected Facebook Page simultaneously.

**Option B — Meta Business Suite (after Instagram publish)**
1. Open Meta Business Suite.
2. Go to Content → Posts.
3. Find the published Instagram Reel.
4. Click Share to Facebook Page.

## HOW TO PUBLISH — FACEBOOK PAGE POST

**Option A — Manual (Facebook App or Desktop)**
1. Go to your Facebook Page.
2. Click Create Post.
3. Upload your image.
4. Paste the caption above into the post body.
5. Add the product link in the post body or as the first comment after publishing.
6. Click Publish or Schedule Post.

**Option B — Meta Business Suite**
1. Open Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com or the app).
2. Select your Facebook Page.
3. Click Create Post → Photo/Video.
4. Upload image.
5. Paste caption.
6. Add product URL in the post body.
7. Set publish time (recommended: Wednesday or Thursday, 9–11 AM or 1–3 PM).
8. Click Schedule or Publish.

**Option C — Later**
1. Open Later (later.com).
2. Click Create → Post → Facebook.
3. Upload image.
4. Paste caption and product link.
5. Set schedule date and time.
6. Click Save and Schedule.

**Option D — Metricool**
1. Open Metricool (metricool.com).
2. Click Planner → New Publication → Facebook.
3. Upload image.
4. Paste caption and product URL.
5. Set date and time.
6. Click Schedule.
