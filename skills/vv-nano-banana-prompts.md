# Skill: vv-nano-banana-prompts
# Vital Vision Shop — Nano Content Prompt Generator
# "Nano" = micro-format, high-impact, single-idea content units
# "Banana" = friendly internal shorthand for quick-turn creative prompts

---

## Purpose
Generate short, punchy, single-idea content prompts for Vital Vision organic posts.
Each prompt is a seed — a hook, a question, or a one-liner — designed to be handed
to vv-organic-content-skill.md or a human creator as a starting point.
Output is a batch of 5–10 nano prompts per session, saved as a draft.

---

## When to Use
- When planning a week of content and need fast creative direction
- When the content calendar has empty slots and needs quick ideas
- When testing new angles before writing full captions
- When the founder wants to batch-record Reels and needs hook options fast

---

## Inputs
- Product focus (Inner Calm / Inner Grow / Inner Bloom / Inner Balance / Quiz Funnel / Brand)
- Goal (Awareness / Quiz clicks / Product education / Trust / Conversion)
- Quantity: how many prompts (default: 5)
- Format: Hook only / Hook + angle / Hook + angle + CTA direction

---

## Outputs
- Numbered list of nano prompts (hooks, angles, one-liners)
- Each prompt tagged with: [format] [product] [goal]
- Saved to: automations/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-nano-prompts-[product-slug].md
- Compliance pre-check note on each prompt

## Safety and Compliance Rules

- Nano prompts must not contain disease claims even as hooks
- No "anxiety," "insomnia," "hair loss," "IBS" as conditions being targeted
- Hooks may reference a feeling ("tired," "off," "overwhelmed") but not a diagnosis
- All prompts must be passable through vv-compliance-guardian before use
- Mark any borderline prompt with: ⚠️ REVIEW BEFORE USE

## Example Prompt

> Use vv-nano-banana-prompts. Product: Inner Calm. Goal: Quiz clicks. Quantity: 5. Format: Hook + angle.

**Expected output:**

1. [Instagram Reel] [Inner Calm] [Quiz clicks]
   Hook: "Your evenings deserve more than just collapsing."
   Angle: Position the evening wind-down ritual. Pivot to quiz.
   ⚠️ REVIEW: Ensure no sleep disorder claim in body copy.

2. [Instagram caption] [Inner Calm] [Awareness]
   Hook: "One small ritual. Every evening."
   Angle: Daily consistency over intensity. One supplement, one step.

3. [Facebook post] [Inner Calm] [Quiz clicks]
   Hook: "Not sure what your body needs most right now?"
   Angle: Decision paralysis. Quiz as the answer.

4. [Reel hook] [Inner Calm] [Trust]
   Hook: "We won't promise you a miracle."
   Angle: Honesty and transparency. Soft benefit language follows.

5. [Story frame] [Quiz Funnel] [Quiz clicks]
   Hook: "Quick question before the week starts..."
   Angle: Sunday check-in. Four options. Quiz link.
