# Skill: Hook Multiplier

## Purpose

Generate premium, emotionally specific, compliance-safe hooks for Vital Vision Shop organic content.

Use this skill for:
- Instagram hooks
- Reels openings
- Story Q&A prompts
- Feed captions
- Email subject lines
- DM automation prompts
- Product education angles

---

## Brand Voice

Vital Vision Shop voice is premium, warm, educational, elegant, and softly persuasive.

Customer-facing content must be simple, clear, elegant English.

Avoid:
- aggressive sales language
- overly medical claims
- miracle promises
- generic messaging
- cheap promotional tones
- fear-based messaging

---

## Compliance Rules

Never use:
- cure
- treat
- heal
- fix
- prevent disease
- diagnose
- guaranteed results
- no more bloating
- fix your gut
- heal your stomach
- cure digestion
- anxiety cure
- insomnia cure
- IBS treatment
- stop hair loss
- detox disease claims

Use instead:
- may support
- helps support
- designed to support
- supports daily digestive wellness
- supports gut balance
- supports digestive comfort
- supports overall wellness
- part of a gentler daily routine
- helps you build a consistent self-care ritual

---

## Hook Categories

Generate hooks in 4 categories:

1. Curiosity hooks
2. Contrarian hooks
3. Specific and numbered hooks
4. Identity and transformation hooks

Each category should produce 5 hooks.

---

## Format Rules

Each hook must:
- be 8 to 14 words
- use no hashtags
- use no emojis
- use no dashes
- avoid the word secret
- avoid AI-sounding words like unlock, unleash, elevate, harness, supercharge, revolutionary
- sound like real customer language
- stay compliant for supplement marketing
