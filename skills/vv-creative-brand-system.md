## Source Priority Order

Use this hierarchy for ALL Vital Vision creative production. Top source wins.

```
1. PUBLIC VITAL VISION STOREFRONT (vitalvision.shop)
   · Homepage, product pages — customer-facing copy, positioning, benefits, CTAs
   · This is the approved customer-facing voice

2. LIVE SHOPIFY PRODUCT DATA
   · Product identity, title, price, handle, status
   · Official Shopify CDN product image URLs (real bottle renders)
   · Short description does NOT override storefront page copy

3. VITAL VISION PROJECT FILES
   · config/brand-voice.md — tone and voice
   · config/product-library.md — organic CTAs, safe positioning, avoid language
   · vital-vision-system/CLAUDE.md — brand/compliance operating guide
   · config/compliance-rules.md — mandatory compliance gate

4. APPROVED PUBLISHING PACK / CANVA BRIEF  (post-specific, locked)
   · Exact approved hook, headline, body copy, CTA for this specific post
   · Do NOT silently rewrite locked approved copy

5. COMPLIANCE RULES  (mandatory gate at every stage)
   · Storefront language is NOT permission to strengthen or extrapolate claims
   · If storefront wording conflicts with compliance rules → STOP and flag Lucy

6. REVERSE-ENGINEERING / COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE  (structure/inspiration only)
   · Hooks, formats, storytelling patterns, visual hierarchy — never copy identity

7. CANVA  (execution layer only)
   · Layout, composition, hierarchy, spacing, visual balance, decorative treatment
   · Canva must NOT invent: copy, claims, product appearance, or brand identity
   · Canva must NOT substitute AI-generated product imagery for real assets
```

## Typography (Verified)

- **Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans / DM Sans / Inter — clean, modern sans-serif
- **Body:** Lightweight sans-serif — airy, readable
- **Avoid:** Script fonts, decorative fonts, anything busy or unserious

## Photography Direction

- Soft natural light — no harsh flash
- Neutral surfaces: white, linen, sage green, warm wood
- Props: water glass, small plant, journal, linen cloth, supplement bottle
- Lifestyle feel preferred over stock-photo look
- **No clinical or medical imagery** (no lab coats, syringes, hospital settings)
- **No before/after imagery**
- **Lifestyle photography: NOT VERIFIED** — no approved lifestyle assets confirmed in project as of 2026-09-02

## What Canva May and May NOT Decide

### Canva MAY decide:
- Layout and composition
- Visual hierarchy and spacing
- Typography treatment within approved brand direction
- Placement of elements
- Approved decorative accents (botanical line art, texture overlays)
- Visual balance and proportion

### Canva must NOT decide or invent:
- Product benefits or claims
- Headlines or body copy
- CTAs or educational statements
- Product positioning or brand identity
- Product appearance (must use real Vital Vision asset)
- Testimonials or results language
- Label or packaging design

- Animation, timing, and transitions (for Reels)
- Overall visual concept and creative structure

**What must NEVER be taken from a template:**
- Any copy, headline, or body text
- Any claim or benefit statement
- Any product name, ingredient, or recommendation
- Any CTA wording
- Any brand identity element
- Any supplement-specific content

**MASTER PRESERVATION RULE:**
The original template MASTER must never be modified.
Always work from a copy. The MASTER is the reusable source.
