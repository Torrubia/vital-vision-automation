# Skill: Customer Language Research

## Purpose

This skill helps Vital Vision Shop turn customer pain research into usable organic content language.

Use this skill to extract:
- real customer phrases
- emotional pain points
- hidden objections
- buying triggers
- organic content angles
- Q&A prompts
- DM prompts
- Reels hooks
- carousel topics
- safe product bridges

---

## Brand Context

Vital Vision Shop creates premium, warm, educational, elegant, and softly persuasive wellness content.

Customer-facing language must be simple, clear, elegant English.

The goal is to make the customer feel:
- understood
- supported
- educated
- not pressured
- invited into a simple daily self-care ritual

---

## Research Input Types

Input may include:
- Reddit posts
- Facebook group comments
- YouTube comments
- Amazon reviews
- customer surveys
- product reviews
- sales calls
- support emails
- social comments
- founder notes
- market research documents

---

## What To Extract

For each product or theme, extract:

1. Customer pain
What does the customer say is happening?

2. Emotional consequence
How does it affect confidence, routine, plans, clothing, meals, energy, beauty, calm, or consistency?

3. Tried and failed solutions
What has the customer already tried?

4. Hidden objection
What belief or fear keeps them from buying or staying consistent?

5. Customer language bank
Exact phrases or natural customer-style phrases.

6. Organic content angle
How can this become content for Reels, Stories, captions, carousels, Pinterest, email, or DM automation?

7. Safe product bridge
How can Vital Vision mention the product as support without making medical claims?

---

## Compliance Rules

Separate customer pain from product promise.

Allowed as customer language:
- I eat and my stomach feels like a balloon.
- I feel heavy after meals.
- I want something simple.
- My routine feels unpredictable.
- I want beauty support from within.
- My evening routine feels inconsistent.

Not allowed as product promise:
- This product stops bloating.
- This product fixes digestion.
- This product cures anxiety.
- This product treats insomnia.
- This product stops hair loss.
- This product guarantees results.

Safe product bridge:
- designed to support
- helps support
- may support
- supports daily wellness
- supports gut balance
- supports a calm routine
- supports beauty from within
- part of a consistent self-care routine
