## Primary Image Generation Prompt

Realistic iPhone lifestyle photo. Warm natural morning light. Organic Instagram feel. Premium but natural. Not retouched. Not a professional photo shoot.

A woman in her late twenties to early thirties in a calm morning wellness moment — standing or sitting at a kitchen counter or bathroom vanity in soft morning light. The mood is quiet, unhurried, and personal. She is mid-routine — maybe she has just placed something on the counter, or is looking down at her morning items thoughtfully. She is not posing. She is not looking at the camera. This is a real moment, not a performance.

Her expression is gentle, natural, and calm — the kind of face someone makes when they are quietly doing something they enjoy. Slight softness to the face. Not smiling for a photo. Not performing wellness.

The setting is a warm, lived-in kitchen counter or bathroom vanity. Soft morning window light falls from the side — golden, warm, not harsh. The surface has a few simple objects: a glass of water, a mug of coffee or tea, maybe a small plant or candle nearby. A bottle of Inner Bloom sits naturally on the counter beside these items — it is part of the scene, not the focus. The label is readable but the bottle is not centred or held up. It is simply there, as it would be in a real morning routine.

The background is softly out of focus — a warm, slightly blurred kitchen or bathroom interior. Cream walls, warm wood tones, linen textures, marble or stone counter surface. Clean but lived-in. Not staged. Not a set.

The image is shot in 9:16 portrait orientation, vertical, filling the full Story frame. The composition gives space at the top and bottom for text overlays to be added in Canva — so the subject and counter are in the middle third of the frame, with breathing room above and below.

Colour palette: warm cream, soft white, sage green, natural wood, warm gold morning light. No cold tones. No neon. No high contrast.

Skin is natural — real texture, warm tone, no airbrushing, no digital smoothing. She looks healthy and real, exactly as she is. Her hair is natural and unperfected — the way it would look in an actual morning moment.

Clothing is simple and calm: a soft robe, a simple linen top, a neutral-toned casual top. Nothing branded. Nothing loud. Simple morning clothing.

## Troubleshooting

**Image looks too AI / too polished:**
Add to prompt: "imperfect, real skin texture, slight natural movement, not retouched, organic feel, looks like a real person's Instagram photo"

**Lucy's appearance looks altered:**
Add to prompt: "match the reference image exactly — do not change facial structure, do not change body proportions, do not change skin tone or ethnicity"

**Product looks like an advertisement:**
Add to prompt: "product is incidental, not the focus, natural placement, sits on counter among everyday objects, not centred, not held up to camera"

**Light is too cold or too bright:**
Add to prompt: "warm golden morning light, soft side window light, warm colour temperature 3000K-4000K, no flash, no overhead studio light"

**Background is too clean / too staged:**
Add to prompt: "real home, lived-in space, slight natural imperfections in background, not a studio set"
