## Purpose

This brief gives Lucy step-by-step instructions to build the master Q&A Story card template in Canva, based on the approved local design (v3/v4 iteration).

Once this master template is built, all future Q&A Story cards are produced by:
1. Duplicating the master (never editing the master directly)
2. Swapping product name, question, answer, and CTA text only
3. Exporting as PNG draft
4. Lucy reviews and approves before use

### ZONE 1 — Header Block

**Decorative gold mark inside header (——◇——):**

## Compliance Checklist (complete before exporting any card)

- [ ] No disease claims on any text element
- [ ] No cure, treat, heal, fix, prevent, diagnose language
- [ ] No guaranteed results language
- [ ] No before-and-after or medical imagery
- [ ] Disclaimer present: "Wellness support only. Not medical advice. Results may vary."
- [ ] Brand signature present: "Self-care starts here."
- [ ] No shame-based language in question or answer copy
- [ ] Product positioned as wellness support only
- [ ] Card tone is warm, educational, softly persuasive — not aggressive

## Duplication Rules for Other Products

This template is designed to be duplicated for Inner Calm, Inner Grow, and Inner Balance with text-only swaps. The visual structure, colours, fonts, and layout do not change between products.

**When duplicating for a new product:**

1. Duplicate the master template in Canva (never edit the master)
2. Rename the duplicate: YYYY-MM-DD-[product]-[theme]-qa-story-draft
3. Swap only these elements:

| Element | Inner Bloom | Inner Calm | Inner Grow | Inner Balance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product label | INNER BLOOM | INNER CALM | INNER GROW | INNER BALANCE |
| Question | Digestive theme | Evening calm / sleep theme | Hair, skin, nails theme | Daily wellness theme |
| Answer | Digestive support copy | Evening calm support copy | Beauty wellness copy | Multivitamin support copy |
| CTA main | COMMENT BLOOM | COMMENT CALM | COMMENT GROW | COMMENT BALANCE |
| CTA sub | for the daily ritual. | for your evening ritual. | for the daily ritual. | for your daily ritual. |

**Do not change:**
- Colours
- Fonts
- Layout zones
- Header style
- Disclaimer text
- Brand signature text
- Card structure

---

## Exporting a Draft Card

1. Complete the card
2. Review against the compliance checklist above
3. Export: File → Download → PNG → 1x
4. File name: YYYY-MM-DD-[product]-[theme]-qa-story-v1-draft.png
5. Save to: assets/generated/qa-cards/
6. Do NOT rename to -approved until Lucy has reviewed and signed off

---

## Approval Gate

Every card built from this template requires Lucy's review before use.

Lucy reviews:
- Colours match palette
- Typography is correct
- All text within safe zones (nothing cut off)
- Copy is compliance-safe
- Disclaimer present and legible
- Brand signature present
- Card feels premium, warm, educational — not salesy

Approved cards: rename to YYYY-MM-DD-[product]-[theme]-qa-story-v1-approved.png
Rejected cards: note rejection reason and rebuild
