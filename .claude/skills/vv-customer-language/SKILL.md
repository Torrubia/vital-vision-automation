# Vital Vision Shop — Customer Language Research

Turn customer pain research into usable organic content language for Vital Vision Shop.

Extract real customer phrases, emotional pain points, hidden objections, buying triggers, and content angles.

---

## Brand Context

Vital Vision Shop creates premium, warm, educational, elegant, and softly persuasive wellness content.

The goal is to make the customer feel:
- understood
- supported
- educated
- not pressured
- invited into a simple daily self-care ritual

---

## Research Input Types

Input may include:
- Reddit posts, Facebook group comments, YouTube comments
- Amazon reviews, customer surveys, product reviews
- Sales calls, support emails, social comments
- Founder notes, market research documents
- Content seed files from `content/drafts/[product]-organic-seed.md`

---

## What To Extract

For each product or theme, extract:

1. **Customer pain** — What does the customer say is happening?
2. **Emotional consequence** — How does it affect confidence, routine, plans, energy, beauty, calm, or consistency?
3. **Tried and failed solutions** — What has the customer already tried?
4. **Hidden objection** — What belief or fear keeps them from buying or staying consistent?
5. **Customer language bank** — Exact phrases or natural customer-style phrases (10 minimum)
6. **Organic content angle** — How can this become content for Reels, Stories, captions, carousels, Pinterest, email, or DM automation?
7. **Safe product bridge** — How can Vital Vision mention the product as support without making medical claims?

---

## Compliance Rules

Separate customer pain from product promise.

**Allowed as customer language (their words):**
- "I eat and my stomach feels like a balloon."
- "I feel heavy after meals."
- "I want something simple."
- "My routine feels unpredictable."
- "I want beauty support from within."
- "My evening routine feels inconsistent."

**Not allowed as product promise:**
- This product stops bloating.
- This product fixes digestion.
- This product cures anxiety.
- This product treats insomnia.
- This product stops hair loss.
- This product guarantees results.

**Safe product bridge language:**
- designed to support
- helps support / may support
- supports daily wellness / gut balance / a calm routine / beauty from within
- part of a consistent self-care routine
